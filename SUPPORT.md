# Support and bug reports

Use the issue forms and include the exact BO1VR version. Describe the last
working action and what happens next. For Campaign include mission/checkpoint;
for Zombies include map, weapon and whether it is Pack-a-Punched.

Include headset, PCVR connection, OpenXR runtime (Auto/VDXR/Meta/SteamVR) and
whether it happens in solo or private co-op. Say whether support grip, reloading,
death/downing, a menu change or a vehicle transition triggers the issue.

For launch failures, run Bo1VR-Check.bat and provide its exact error and relevant
compatibility report. A Discord warning before downloading is not a Windows
launch error. Keep security protection enabled; if Windows detects a file,
include its exact detection name and the release asset name.

Review logs before posting and remove account identifiers, private paths and
other personal data. Do not upload game archives, your Steam files, saves,
private pose caches or access tokens. Relevant excerpts or a short clip are
usually sufficient. Preserve a copy of your settings before troubleshooting.

Known limits: experimental test builds, no universal crash-freedom claim,
physical headset and full Campaign acceptance remain ongoing.
